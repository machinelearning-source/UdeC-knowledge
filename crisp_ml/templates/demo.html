About the re-ranker
This demo scores the fragments that KnowBot retrieves for a question:

  1. Candidates: the 25 closest fragments by meaning, the 15 best keyword matches and the exact calendar and article matches.
  2. Features: 23 measures of how well each fragment matches the question and its page.
  3. Model: a Random Forest gives each fragment the probability that it comes from the page that answers the question.
  4. Result: the five best fragments, next to KnowBot's current order, with document and page.

Try the model

Ask a question about the University of Cundinamarca documents. The model scores KnowBot's candidate fragments and shows the five it ranks first, next to KnowBot's current order.
