User documentation

How to use KnowBot Desk, the private system that manages academic inquiries with the help of the Machine Learning model. For quick questions without an account, use the KnowBot chatbot.

Getting started

  1. Open the homepage and choose Register (top right). New accounts always have the Student role.
  2. Sign in with your email and password. The KnowBot Desk button opens your dashboard; Sign out closes the session.
  3. Advisor and Coordinator accounts are created by a coordinator, who assigns the role in Users and roles.
  4. If the site was idle, the first visit can take about a minute while the free server wakes up.

Complete workflow

  1. Student: New inquiry → title, category and question (15 to 500 characters, English or Spanish) → Send inquiry.
  2. Model: the re-ranker scores the pages of the official documents and stores the inference (date, user, result, model version). If its top probability is below 0.30 the inquiry is Escalated to the coordinator; otherwise it is New in the advisors' queue.
  3. Advisor: opens the inquiry, reads the five suggested pages with their probability and excerpt, presses Take this inquiry, selects the page that really supports the answer (or writes another document and page) and sends the answer. An advisor can also send an inquiry to the coordinator.
  4. Student: reads the answer and the confirmed source and rates it from 1 to 5; the inquiry is Closed.
  5. Coordinator: follows the indicators in Reports: inquiries by status and category, response time, ratings and how often the model's page was confirmed.

How to read the model's result

  • Meaning: the probability that a fragment comes from the page that answers the question.
  • Use: it pre-selects the page the advisor checks first and routes unsure cases to the coordinator.
  • Limitations: on the test set the model put the right page first in 55% of the questions and among the first three in 75%; Spanish questions are somewhat weaker. The advisor always confirms or corrects the page.

Incidents and feedback

  • Rate every answer and leave a comment; ratings and confirmed pages are reviewed in Reports.
  • Report problems (wrong date or requirement, errors, access issues) as an issue in the project repository. A wrong deadline or requirement is handled first, within one week.
  • The team reviews the monitoring indicators weekly during the semester.

Training: a 10-minute walkthrough

  1. Register as a student and create an inquiry about the minimum group size.
  2. Sign in as an advisor, take it, check the suggested page (Undergraduate Student Regulations, page 6) and answer.
  3. Sign in again as the student, read the answer and rate it.
  4. Sign in as the coordinator and open Reports and Model history to see the inference and the confirmed page.
