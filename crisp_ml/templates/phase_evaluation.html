4. Model Evaluation

Metrics, cross-validation comparison, overfitting and risk analysis, the team's assessment of the errors, model selection and the final result on the independent test set.

Metrics and why

MetricDefinitionWhy
MRR (selection metric)Mean of 1 / position of the first fragment from the expected page (0 if absent)KnowBot reads the fragments in order; the earlier the right page, the better the answer. One number that rewards first place most.
Hit@1, Hit@3Share of questions with the expected page first / in the first threeEasy to explain to stakeholders: "how often the right page is on top".
Page ±1 at rank 1Like Hit@1 but the neighbouring page also countsA rule can start at the end of one page and continue on the next.
PR-AUC, ROC-AUCClassification quality over all rowsWith {{ '%.1f'|format(quality.positive_rate * 100) }}% positives, accuracy would be misleading; PR-AUC focuses on the rare positives.

Overfitting, underfitting and bias

  • Random Forest and Gradient Boosting overfit: deep trees reach a training MRR far above validation (selected model: {{ '%.3f'|format(test.selected_model_on_training_data.mrr) }} on its training data vs {{ '%.3f'|format(card.cv_validation_mrr_mean) }} in validation). Larger leaves (5 samples) and shallow boosting were the best settings, which confirms that the data favors simple models.
  • Logistic Regression does not overfit (training and validation MRR are close) but cannot use interactions; with this little data it is almost as good.
  • High variance between folds: the standard deviation of MRR (about 0.14) is larger than the gap between the best models and KnowBot's order. With 16 questions per fold, one question moves MRR by several points.
  • Bias by document: the calendar has 3 questions in total and the Commission report 11, so the model mostly learned from the two regulations. Results for those two documents are less reliable.
  • Label bias: labels mark one page; when the neighbouring page also answers, the model is counted wrong (see "Page ±1").

Result: on the test questions the selected model does not beat KnowBot's current order (MRR {{ '%.3f'|format(test.selected_model.mrr) }} vs {{ '%.3f'|format(test.knowbot_baseline.mrr) }}); it ties plain vector search in Hit@1 and Hit@3. The validation gain did not generalize. Each test question is worth 5 points of Hit@1, so the gap (one question at rank 1) is within the noise of this sample, but the honest conclusion is that the improvement is not confirmed. That is why Phase 5 deploys the model in shadow mode: it runs and is logged next to KnowBot's order, without replacing it, until more labeled questions confirm a gain.
