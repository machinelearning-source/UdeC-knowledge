Detecting data drift and performance degradation

  • Input drift: question length, share of date questions, and the documents that come first are compared with the training data. A new topic (for example a question about postgraduate fees) shows up as lower confidence and a document shift.
  • Corpus change: a new or changed PDF (the 2027 calendar) changes the candidates. KnowBot's index workflow already rebuilds the index when a PDF changes; that event triggers a re-run of build_dataset.py and train.py.
  • Performance: labels arrive from feedback and from team review. Every month, the newly labeled questions are scored with the model and with KnowBot's order.

ActivityFrequencyResponsible

Retrain when: a document is added or changed; 50 or more new labeled questions are available; an alert stays active for two weekly reviews; or MRR on new labeled questions falls below KnowBot's order.

Risks, quality controls and update procedure

  • Risk: a wrong page ranked first with high confidence. Control: shadow mode until a new test confirms a gain, and the gradual switch only above probability 0.5.
  • Risk: training and serving compute features differently. Control: one shared module (knowbot/features.py) and the full pipeline saved in one file.
  • Risk: silent errors. Control: every prediction and error is logged, plus a health endpoint.
  • Update procedure: (1) rebuild the dataset; (2) retrain with the same split rules and keep a new held-out test set; (3) compare with the current model and with KnowBot's order; (4) update model_card.json and the version in the repository; (5) deploy; (6) watch the indicators for one week.
